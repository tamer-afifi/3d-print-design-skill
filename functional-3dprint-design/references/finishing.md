# Finishing guide

Include this guide when the part is cosmetic. Paint and primer add thickness, so do every
**fit check before finishing** and **mask the fit surfaces** before any coat goes on.

## Order of work

1. **Test-fit everything** with the raw print. Fix fit problems now, not after paint.
2. **Mask fit surfaces.** Pockets, grooves, peg holes, clip posts, screen ledges and anything
   that slides or snaps. Use painter's tape or plug holes with foam / blu-tack.
3. **Sand dry** with **220** grit to knock down layer lines, then **320**. Sand along the
   layer lines and keep edges crisp. Don't round them off.
4. **Filler primer.** 2–3 light coats from 25–30 cm, 10–15 minutes apart. It fills layer lines.
   Let it cure fully (follow the can; usually a few hours).
5. **Wet-sand** with **400**, then **600**. Repeat steps 4–5 until no layer lines show under a
   raking light.
6. **Colour coats.** 2–3 light coats (black metallic worked well on PitVista). Avoid runs.
   Light coats are better than heavy ones.
7. **Clear coat.** 2 coats of **satin clear** for durability and an even sheen.
8. **Cure** at least 24 hours before handling heavily or assembling tight fits.
9. **Remove masking** and test-fit again. Clean up any paint edges with a sharp blade.

## Fabric wrap (optional)

For a speaker-grille or "premium" look on flat or gently curved panels:

1. Cut fabric with 15–20 mm overlap on every edge.
2. Spray **3M Super 77** (or similar spray adhesive) lightly on the **part**, not the fabric.
3. Lay the fabric from the centre outward, smoothing as you go.
4. Wrap the edges to the inside, relief-cut corners, and glue the overlap.
5. Trim openings with a fresh blade **after** the adhesive sets.

## Acrylic bezel / front panel (last)

Fit any acrylic bezel, lens or front panel **last**, after paint has cured and the electronics
are tested. That keeps it free of overspray and fingerprints. Use the protective film until
the very end.

## Safety

Sand primer wet or wear a dust mask. Spray paint and adhesive outdoors or with ventilation and
a respirator rated for organic vapours.
